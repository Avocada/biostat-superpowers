# Public CPTAC-CCRCC input snapshot
Matrices: https://www.linkedomics.org/data_download/CPTAC-CCRCC/
Proteins are processed TMT log2 reference ratios; RNA is supplied log2 FPKM. Do not log-transform again, assume raw counts, or subtract assay values across molecular layers as though identically calibrated.
Original study: Clark et al., Cell 2019, PMID 31675502, PMCID PMC7331093. Published main cohort:103 ccRCC tumors/80 NAT; RNA NAT72. Download includes110 tumors/84 protein NAT/75 RNA NAT.
Clinical_Table_S1.xlsx: public CPTAC maintainer index https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PayneLab/cptac/v0.9.7/cptac/data_ccrcc/index_urls.tsv -> https://byu.box.com/shared/static/uc9tr2tpvn71feueg50oofkd4c32ri7g.txt -> https://byu.box.com/shared/static/q5bayxdevpwmq74chay5m0lrnc6vb71i.xlsx . MD5 matches published 2cb05a2134b8345793629cf6399d76c3. clinical_annotation.csv is sheet ccrcc_clinical_characteristics, nonempty case rows, empty trailing columns omitted. Use this authoritative histology/stage annotation over the older nine-field CLI.tsi export; values can differ.
cptac_metadata.csv.gz: https://byu.box.com/shared/static/mf5uzbi0p1yldywbebrybavxdfg9vaqy.gz (maintainer index). Maps specimen CPT0012090003 to C3N-00314 normal. The supplementary sample-availability sheet excludes that NAT for tumor contamination. Exclude its normal tissue/pair, not silently every tumor record.
Seven non-ccRCC cases per authoritative histology: C3L-00359, C3N-00313, C3N-00435, C3N-00492, C3N-00832, C3N-01175, C3N-01180. Check these against supplied metadata. Cross-modal cohort requires a matched case in all four expression matrices, after exclusions; do not treat columns as independent participants.
Inputs are host-downloaded public processed data, not downloaded by MCP. Do not claim this reproduces raw mass-spectrometry or sequencing processing. Exact processing provenance, batch effects, adjacent tissue biology and clinical confounding remain limitations. No survival or treatment-response endpoint is supplied. Do not infer drug efficacy.
