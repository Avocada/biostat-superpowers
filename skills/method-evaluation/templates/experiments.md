# Experimental Method Evaluation Template

Use for randomized experiments, factorial designs, blocking, response-surface methods, measurement systems, process improvement, and designed comparisons.

## Specify

- Experimental unit, treatment factors, levels, response, randomization unit, blocking, replication, and run order.
- Primary contrast or estimand.
- Measurement protocol and whether the outcome was blinded or automated.
- Constraints, nuisance factors, and operational setting.

## Assumptions

- Assignment was randomized or design restrictions are documented.
- Experimental units are independent unless blocked or clustered.
- Measurement error is acceptable and not treatment-dependent.
- Interference, carryover, and contamination are absent or modeled.
- Factorial or response-surface conclusions respect interactions and design resolution.

## Diagnostics

- Randomization integrity and baseline balance.
- Attrition, noncompliance, protocol deviations, and missing outcomes.
- Residuals, variance homogeneity, normality where needed, and influential runs.
- Interaction plots for factorial designs.
- Measurement-system repeatability and reproducibility when measurement is fragile.

## Stress Tests

- Per-protocol versus intention-to-treat framing when noncompliance exists.
- Robust or permutation inference for small or non-normal experiments.
- Excluding failed runs only with documented criteria.
- Interaction inclusion/exclusion sensitivity.
- Blocking and nuisance-factor sensitivity.

## Compare

- Planned contrasts before exploratory contrasts.
- Main-effects-only versus interaction models when design supports it.
- Parametric versus randomization/permutation inference for small samples.

## Improve

- Add blocking, replication, randomization checks, or measurement-system analysis.
- Clarify primary endpoint and multiplicity control.
- Reduce claims when interactions or protocol deviations weaken interpretation.
