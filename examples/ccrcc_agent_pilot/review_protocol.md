The estimand is the mean within-participant tumor-minus-matched-normal difference on each assay's documented processed scale among eligible observable pairs; explicitly distinguish this observed-sample estimand from a claim about all CCRCC patients.

Freeze identical inputs and checksums, question, output requirements, follow-up prompt, model/settings, allowed effort/time, and starting external evidence date where feasible. ## Mandatory scientific checks and major pitfalls

1. **Cohort and exclusions — high, critical if misrepresented.** Reconcile 110 versus paper 103 using source metadata and the publication's stated exclusions; do not silently delete seven patients, equate sample count with patients, or claim exact paper replication. Require counts for downloaded, unique, eligible, paired per assay, jointly paired, and analyzed. If exclusion identities/reasons cannot be established, label the download-based cohort and its difference from the publication. Owner: data preparation.
2. **Pairing — critical.** Tumor and normal must match verified participant identifiers; order or visually similar column names are insufficient. Technical aliquots are not independent patients. Require duplicate/ambiguous mapping checks and a paired design or explicit analysis of within-person differences. Preserve whole participants in resampling. Owner: preparation and statistical analysis.
3. **Processed scales — high.** Establish whether RNA is log2(expression plus offset), protein is a log2 reference ratio, or another normalization; already logged values must not be logged again. Tumor-minus-normal differences may be valid within an assay while absolute cross-assay levels are incomparable. Call a contrast a log2 fold change only when the transformation/reference supports that interpretation; disclose offsets. Protein reference cancellation requires compatible reference and processing. Do not subtract RNA and protein effects without addressing their scale and calibration. Owner: preparation and analysis.
4. **Missingness and gene-wise n — high.** A gene contributes only participants with both tissue values for that assay; integration requires the stated common participants. Report paired n for every tested gene and all discordance candidates, missingness by assay/tissue, and exact eligibility thresholds. Never replace undetected protein with zero on a log scale or treat absence of detection as established biological absence. Complete-pair selection may be abundance dependent; discuss selection and test defensible sensitivity policies rather than presenting imputation as automatic repair. Owner: preparation/missing-data and analysis.
5. **Model and multiple testing — high.** Use a paired method compatible with processed continuous values, not a raw-count likelihood applied to log data. Report contrast sign, effect, interval, p, q, n, failures, and test specification. Declare the BH family and denominator for RNA, protein, direct discordance and each enrichment collection; adjustment after selecting favorable genes is invalid. Separate assay-specific FDR control does not imply global FDR for every combined claim. Filtering must be fixed and defensible without choosing favorable effect/p-value results. Owner: statistical analysis.
6. **Discordance — critical if causal or false inferential claim.** “Significant in RNA, nonsignificant in protein” is not evidence of a significant RNA–protein difference. Opposite estimated signs are descriptive unless uncertainty supports the statement. Distinguish differential-response discordance, cross-patient correlation of paired changes, and absolute tumor abundance correlation. If testing a cross-assay contrast, require a scientifically meaningful comparable scale and a direct model accounting for within-patient covariance; otherwise retain separate effects/intervals and an explicitly descriptive prioritization rule. No inference of post-transcriptional regulation from these associations alone. Owner: analysis and reporting.
7. **Evidence independence — high.** For every shortlisted gene, record identifier, direction/effect/n, ranking rule, source identifier/URL, retrieval date, quoted or precisely summarized support, evidence type and limitations. The original CPTAC paper, a portal of the same cohort, and a paper reanalyzing it are not three independent validations. Database associations, predicted targets and broad pathway membership are not experimental efficacy evidence. Report negative/ambiguous evidence and source failures. Owner: evidence workflow/reporting.
8. **Pathway background — high.** Overrepresentation background must match the eligible mapped/tested genes for the relevant assay or integration rule, not automatically the entire genome. Record database version, mapping/deduplication, overlap sizes, direction rule, collection and BH family. Ranked enrichment requires the full eligible ranked list, an appropriate signed statistic and method assumptions; overlapping gene sets are not independent confirmations. Owner: analysis/reporting.

## Diagnostics and stress tests to request, not implement here

Require distributions of paired differences and sample-level QC sufficient to reveal gross labeling/batch issues; influential-patient checks for the highlighted genes; alternative defensible pair-coverage thresholds; primary-versus-common-patient assay estimates; and sensitivity to published exclusions if recoverable. Choose a robust paired alternative for highlighted conclusions when outliers or skew threaten the primary mean-based result, stating that tests can target different nulls/estimands. Assess shortlist direction/rank stability rather than counting only how many q-values cross 0.05. Unavailable batch/purity/cell-composition information must remain an explicit limitation, not an asserted adjustment. Tumor–normal differences can reflect cell composition, field effects and tissue context.

## Frozen visual rubric

Expect a participant/assay flow summary; missingness or coverage view; separate assay effect-versus-evidence plots; RNA-versus-protein paired-effect plot with units, common-set definition, and descriptive quadrants; and paired observations or effect intervals for the final shortlist. Equivalent figures are acceptable; quantity is not rewarded. Check axes and transformations against exported numbers, no misleading shared scale, q versus p clearly distinguished, zero/reference lines correct, legible labels, accessible color distinctions, exact n or its range, and transparent clipping/filtering. Heatmaps must disclose centering/scaling and selection; row-standardized colors cannot support magnitude comparisons across genes. Do not infer precision from density or omit uncertain candidates merely for visual neatness.

Record correct facts, material errors, unsupported assertions, source reacquisition, reproducibility, decision retrieval, elapsed effort and tool failures. Judge correctness against frozen source/artifact evidence, not memory fluency. Neither agent may see the other's output. Analysis authors own fixes; this reviewer only evaluates supplied artifacts and does not fit models or inspect the other study outputs.
